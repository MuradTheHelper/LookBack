Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about LookBack

🚀 Getting Started

What is LookBack?

LookBack is a beautiful countdown app for iOS that helps you track important events, holidays, and personal milestones. Never miss a special moment again with customizable countdowns and smart notifications.

Is LookBack free to use?

Yes! LookBack offers a free version with access to:

  • Up to 5 personal events
  • 1 country selection for holidays
  • Basic widget styles
  • Standard notifications

Upgrade to Pro for unlimited events, countries, and premium features!

What devices support LookBack?

LookBack requires iOS 14.0 or later and is compatible with:

  • iPhone 6s and newer
  • iPad Air 2 and newer
  • iPad mini 4 and newer
  • iPod touch (7th generation)

📱 Features & Functionality

What types of events can I track?

You can track three main types of events:

  • Personal Events: Birthdays, anniversaries, vacations, deadlines
  • International Holidays: Global celebrations and awareness days
  • Country-Specific Events: National holidays from your selected countries

How do notifications work?

LookBack sends smart notifications to remind you of upcoming events. You can:

  • Set custom reminder times for personal events
  • Receive discovery notifications for interesting global events
  • Get H-1 notifications for tomorrow's events
  • Customize notification preferences in Settings

Can I customize the app's appearance?

Absolutely! LookBack offers extensive customization:

  • Multiple color themes
  • Dark and light mode support
  • Grid or list layout options
  • Premium fonts (with Pro upgrade)
  • Custom icons for your events

How do widgets work?

LookBack widgets let you see your countdowns right on your home screen:

  • Add widgets in multiple sizes
  • Customize which events appear
  • Automatic updates throughout the day
  • Pro users get access to all widget styles

🛠️ Troubleshooting

I'm not receiving notifications. What should I do?

Check these settings:

  1. Open iOS Settings > Notifications > LookBack
  2. Ensure "Allow Notifications" is enabled
  3. Check notification style preferences
  4. Verify Do Not Disturb isn't blocking notifications
  5. Restart the app and try again

My widgets aren't updating. How can I fix this?

Try these solutions:

  1. Remove and re-add the widget
  2. Open the LookBack app to refresh data
  3. Restart your device
  4. Check that you have available storage space
  5. Update to the latest app version

The app is crashing. What should I do?

Follow these steps:

  1. Force close and reopen the app
  2. Restart your device
  3. Update to the latest iOS version
  4. Update LookBack to the latest version
  5. Free up device storage space
  6. Contact support if the issue persists

Some holidays are showing incorrect dates. Why?

Holiday dates are sourced from reliable third-party providers, but occasionally:

  • Different regions may celebrate on different dates
  • Some holidays use lunar calendars with varying dates
  • Government changes can affect official holiday dates

Report any persistent issues to our support team.

🔒 Privacy & Security

What data does LookBack collect?

LookBack prioritizes your privacy:

  • Stored locally: Your personal events and settings stay on your device
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data to improve the app
  • No personal info: We don't collect names, emails, or contacts

Read our Privacy Policy for complete details.

Is my data backed up?

Your LookBack data is automatically included in your device's backup:

  • iCloud Backup: Restores when you set up a new device
  • iTunes/Finder Backup: Included in computer backups

Currently, LookBack doesn't offer its own cloud sync, but this feature is planned for future updates.

Can I delete my data?

Yes! You have full control:

  • Delete individual events within the app
  • Reset all data in Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Contact us to delete any analytics data
