Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Summary

LookBack is a countdown app made by an independent developer. Your countdowns stay on your device. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features people use. That data is not linked to your name or email, is not used for advertising or tracking, and is never sold.

This policy explains exactly what the LookBack iOS app (the "App") collects, why, and what you can do about it. "We" and "us" refer to the developer of LookBack.

2. What Stays on Your Device

The following is stored only on your iPhone, inside the App and its home screen widgets. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your personal countdowns: titles, descriptions, dates, icons, colors and alert settings
  • Your settings: selected countries, themes, fonts, layout and widget styles
  • Notifications: all reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device

This data is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have them turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the App deletes this data from your device.

3. Analytics We Collect

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect usage information so we can see which features are used and fix what isn't working. It collects:

  • An app-instance identifier: a random ID created when the App is installed. It is not your name, email, or Apple Account, and a new one is created if you reinstall the App.
  • How you use the App: for example which screens and categories you open, which countries you select, that a countdown was created or deleted (but not what it says), which widget style, theme or layout you choose, your choices during onboarding, whether you allowed notifications, which public holidays you add from the built-in list, and which built-in search suggestions you tap.
  • Device information: device model, iOS version, App version, language and time zone.
  • Approximate location: Google may estimate your country or city from your IP address. We never receive your IP address or your precise location.

We do not collect the titles or contents of your personal countdowns, the text you type into search, your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or your device's advertising identifier. The App does not ask for permission to track you and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Analytics data is retained by Google for a limited period (no more than 14 months) and then deleted automatically. Google processes it as our service provider under the Firebase privacy terms.

4. Purchases

LookBack Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The App only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock Pro features and restore them on your other devices.

5. How We Use Information

  • To understand which features are useful and which need work
  • To find and fix bugs
  • To decide what to build next

We do not use any information for advertising, we do not build profiles of individual users, and we do not sell or rent information to anyone.

6. Sharing

Analytics data is processed by Google as described above. Apart from that, we only disclose information if we are legally required to.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Notifications: turn off "Discover Global Events" in the App's Settings to stop suggestions about world events, or turn off all notifications for LookBack in iOS Settings > Notifications.
  • Your countdowns: edit or delete them at any time in the App. Deleting the App removes all of them from your device.
  • Analytics: deleting the App stops all collection, and reinstalling it starts over with a new identifier.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because the analytics data we hold is not linked to your name or email, we usually cannot identify which records belong to you, but you can still contact us and we will help where we can.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International Processing

Google may process analytics data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under the safeguards described in its privacy terms.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we change how the App handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the App's release notes.
