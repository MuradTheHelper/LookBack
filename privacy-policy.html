Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 15, 2024

1. Introduction

LookBack ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application LookBack (the "App").

By using our App, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Personal Events Data: When you create personal countdown events, we collect:

  • Event titles and descriptions
  • Event dates and times
  • Custom event settings (themes, icons, notifications)
  • Event categories and tags

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Device Information:

  • Device model and iOS version
  • App version and build number
  • Device timezone and language settings
  • Unique device identifiers (for analytics only)

Usage Analytics:

  • App launch and usage frequency
  • Feature usage statistics (anonymized)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Performance metrics

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal contact information (name, email, phone) unless voluntarily provided for support
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Photos or camera access
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Financial or payment information (handled by Apple)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

3.1 Core App Functionality

  • Store and display your personal events and countdowns
  • Send notifications for upcoming events
  • Provide widget functionality on your home screen
  • Synchronize settings across app launches

3.2 App Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features
  • Identify and fix bugs or performance issues
  • Develop new features based on user behavior
  • Optimize app performance and user experience

3.3 Customer Support

  • Respond to your support requests and questions
  • Troubleshoot technical issues
  • Provide app usage assistance

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

Your personal events and app settings are primarily stored locally on your device using:

  • iOS UserDefaults for app preferences
  • Local Core Data database for events
  • iOS Keychain for secure premium activation data

4.2 Cloud Services

We use Firebase Analytics for anonymized usage statistics. This service is GDPR and CCPA compliant.

4.3 Security Measures

  • Data encryption in transit and at rest
  • Secure API communications (HTTPS only)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Minimal data collection principle
  • No server-side storage of personal event data

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We Do NOT Share Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

5.2 Limited Third-Party Services

Firebase Analytics (Google): We share anonymized usage data for app improvement purposes only. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Apple Services: Notifications and widgets use Apple's services subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect our rights and safety.

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Access and Control

  • View Data: All your data is accessible within the app
  • Modify Data: Edit or delete personal events anytime
  • Export Data: No built-in export feature (data is locally stored)
  • Delete Data: Uninstalling the app removes all local data

6.2 Privacy Controls

  • Disable analytics in app settings
  • Control notification permissions in iOS Settings
  • Manage widget data access
  • Opt-out of discovery notifications

6.3 Regional Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

If you are in the EU or California, you have additional rights:

  • Right to access personal data
  • Right to rectification (correction)
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our App is suitable for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@lookbackapp.com.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data is primarily stored locally on your device. Any analytics data shared with Firebase may be processed in various countries where Google operates data centers. These transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards.

9. Data Retention

  • Personal Events: Stored locally until you delete them or uninstall the app
  • Analytics Data: Retained by Firebase for up to 26 months (anonymized)
  • Support Communications: Retained for 2 years for quality assurance
  • Crash Reports: Automatically deleted after 90 days

10. Third-Party Links and Services

Our App may contain links to third-party websites or integrate with third-party services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those services. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Third-Party Services Used:

  • Firebase Analytics: Google Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple Services: Apple Privacy Policy

11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our mobile app does not use cookies or web tracking technologies. We only collect data as described in this Privacy Policy through standard iOS APIs.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on our website
  • Updating the "Last updated" date
  • Providing in-app notifications for material changes

Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

13. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of personal information collected
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service regardless of privacy choices

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@lookbackapp.com.

Privacy Officer: privacy@lookbackapp.com

Mailing Address:

For EU residents, you can contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@lookbackapp.com for questions about data processing and your rights under GDPR.

16. Consent

By using LookBack, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described herein.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by uninstalling the app or contacting us to request data deletion.

This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we protect your information while providing you with a great countdown app experience.
